Printer troubleshooting - remote checks

Demo employee self-service guidance for print failures when you are working from your computer, without physical access to the printer. This is not a remote-control procedure or model-specific repair guide.

Before starting

Identify the intended printer or queue, your operating system, the symptom, and its impact. Record only the exact error message, not document contents, credentials, or other employees' print jobs. Perform one check at a time.

Checks from your computer

  1. Open the application's normal print dialog and verify that the intended printer or queue is selected, rather than a PDF or virtual printer.
  2. For a workplace network printer, confirm that you are using your normal approved workplace network or an already-configured workplace VPN. Do not install a VPN, change network settings, or disable security controls.
  3. Inspect your own print job using the operating system's normal queue UI. Record whether it is pending or shows an error; do not inspect other jobs.
  4. If your own job is stuck or duplicated, agree before cancelling only that job in the normal UI. Do not clear a shared queue, restart print services, or change another employee's jobs. If cancellation needs elevated permissions, stop and contact the service desk.
  5. If the printer appears ready and no test is outstanding, agree on a single non-sensitive test print. Wait for confirmation before claiming restoration. Do not repeatedly submit documents or create duplicate printouts.

When to switch paths or escalate

If the device's status cannot be established remotely and you can safely visit it, ask to switch to Local checks and follow Printer troubleshooting - local checks. Otherwise contact the service desk with the printer/queue, error, impact, checks, and outcomes.

Escalate access, driver, persistent connectivity, or hardware problems. Do not install software, run commands, provide credentials, or obtain administrator rights. Any incident creation or update requires a separate review and explicit confirmation; troubleshooting does not close a ticket.
