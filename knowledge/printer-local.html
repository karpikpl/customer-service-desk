Printer troubleshooting - local checks

Demo employee self-service guidance for safe observations while you are already at the printer. This is not authorization to repair equipment, replace consumables, or interrupt a shared device.

Before starting

Identify the intended printer or queue, symptom, and business impact. Ask only for the visible status or error, not document contents, credentials, or another employee's printouts. Perform one observation at a time.

Checks at the printer

  1. Read the visible printer or asset label and compare it with the intended queue. Record a mismatch rather than changing device configuration.
  2. Read the visible display or status indicator. Record the exact message or code without opening covers or touching internal components.
  3. If a paper, toner, jam, cover, power, or hardware warning is shown, record it and contact the service desk. Do not open panels, clear jams, handle consumables, move the device, touch cables, or restart it.
  4. If the printer shows ready and you have already requested one non-sensitive test print, check whether your own output arrived. Do not inspect, collect, or disclose another person's printouts.
  5. If no test print is pending, record the visible device state. Contact the service desk if the issue persists; do not guess device-specific controls or repeatedly request test prints.

When to switch paths or escalate

Report the printer label, exact display message, impact, observations, and outcomes when escalating. Keep this procedure focused on local observations at the printer; computer and queue checks are separate.

Do not run commands, install software, request credentials, obtain administrator rights, or perform repairs. Confirm restoration explicitly. Any incident creation, update, or closure requires its own review and confirmation; observing the device does not change an incident.
